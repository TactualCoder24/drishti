Step 1 · What is going wrong

Slow, distorted signals hurt service more than capacity or forecasting

Service fell from 94% to 86% while stock rose from 39 to 46 days. More stock did not raise service, so stock is not the root problem.

₹185 Cr excess and ₹73 Cr shortfall coexist. North and West hold 76% of it.
₹140 Cr (69%) of the ₹202 Cr margin gap is supply-chain leakage.
Overlooked: Pilot B's inventory win moved stock into ACPL warehouses (+7 days), so owned cash got worse.

Honest supply: allocation when supply is short

edit bias and supply

Today the centre cuts every regional forecast 15%. Under the new rule a region's own 13-week bias sets its haircut: within ±3% gets none, rising to 25% at +15% bias or worse.

Flow Contract

replaces quarterly volume slabs

13-week rolling run-rate slab plus a stock-truth rebate (±5% of audit). ₹9 Cr margin guarantee for 6 quarters while distributors adjust.

Three new rules

RuleWhat changes
Flow Contract13-week rolling run-rate slab and a stock-truth rebate (±5% of audit) replace quarterly slabs; ₹9 Cr guarantee for 6 quarters; last-3-week share from 41% to 27% or less.
Honest supplyForecast is not the target. 13-week bias sets the haircut when supply is short: calibrated regions 0%, biased up to 25%, replacing the blanket 15% cut.
Launch gateLaunches need 12-week supply sign-off; SKUs with under 12 months of history are made to order in lead regions; half of year-1 obsolescence is charged to the brand P&L.
Lead indicators to watch: order loop 11.9 to 7 days or less · quarter-end loading 1.78× to 1.2× or less · distributor stock-truth gap 12% to 5% or less · general-trade forecast bias within ±3%.

About the numbers

The model uses monthly cash flows over 60 months, values working capital at 11% a year, certifies benefits one quarter after delivery and treats pilot costs as sunk. Figures are estimates that you can test on the Returns and risk desk. Background: Lee, Padmanabhan & Whang (1997) on demand distortion; precedents draw on Hammond (HBS), iSixSigma Asia, HUL (2018), Danone (vendor-reported) and DeHoratius & Raman (2008); ACPL case Appendices 1–6 for the source data.
